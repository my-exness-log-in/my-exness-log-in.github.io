Introduction

Exness login is the authentication process that grants you access to your account with one of the world's largest online forex and CFD brokers. Founded in 2008 and serving traders in over 180 countries, Exness has built a reputation for instant withdrawals, competitive spreads starting from 0.0 pips, and leverage options that go up to 1:Unlimited on qualifying accounts. Understanding how Exness login works is the single most important step before you can manage your funds, open trading positions, or configure your account settings. This guide covers every aspect of Exness login — what it is, how it works across all platforms, the security features that protect your account, common problems and their fixes, and practical tips for keeping your login safe. ⁠

What Exactly Is Exness Login?
Exness login is not a single, simple "enter email and password" process. It is a two-tier authentication system that separates your account management from your trading execution. ⁠ This design is deliberate: it means that even if someone somehow obtains your trading password, they still cannot access your deposits, withdrawals, or account settings without your Personal Area credentials — and vice versa.

The two tiers are:

Tier 1 — Personal Area (PA) Login. This is your central administrative dashboard, accessible at my.exness.com or through the Exness Trade mobile app. ⁠ You log in with your registered email address and your Personal Area password. ⁠ From the Personal Area you can deposit and withdraw funds, verify your identity (KYC), open new trading accounts, change leverage limits, transfer money between accounts, manage security settings, and launch the browser-based Exness Terminal. One Personal Area login gives you access to up to 200 individual trading accounts across all account types — Standard, Cent, Pro, Raw Spread, and Zero. ⁠

Tier 2 — Trading Platform Login. This is how you connect to MetaTrader 4 (MT4) or MetaTrader 5 (MT5) on desktop, mobile, or web. ⁠ You log in with a numeric trading account number (an 8- or 9-digit ID like 148920341), a separate trading password, and the exact server name (for example, "Exness-MT5Real14" for a live account or "Exness-MT5Trial8" for a demo). ⁠ The trading password is completely independent of your Personal Area password. Changing one does not affect the other. ⁠

The single most common Exness login error is entering your Personal Area password into MT4 or MT5, which produces an "Authorization Failed" message. ⁠ If you are seeing that error, stop and check that you are using the correct password for the correct platform.

One important exception: if your MT5 account was automatically created during registration, the initial trading password is the same as your Personal Area password. ⁠ For all other accounts, the trading password is set separately and can be changed independently from the Personal Area under Trading → Accounts → three-dot menu → "Change Trading Password." ⁠

How to Log In to the Exness Personal Area (Web)
The Personal Area is the primary login destination for most users. Here is the exact step-by-step process:

Open your web browser and type my.exness.com/accounts/sign-in directly into the address bar. ⁠ Do not click on login links in emails, Telegram messages, WhatsApp messages, or search advertisements. Always verify that the URL shows "my.exness.com" and that there is a padlock icon in the address bar, confirming a secure HTTPS connection. ⁠
Enter your registered email address in the username field. ⁠ This is the email you used when you first created your Exness account. A trading account number is not a valid Personal Area username. Do not enter your name or phone number here. ⁠
Enter your Personal Area password. The password is case-sensitive and must be at least 8 characters long, consisting of both letters and numbers. ⁠ You can click the "eye" icon in the password field to verify what you have typed. Check that Caps Lock is off and that there are no stray spaces. ⁠
Complete two-factor authentication (2FA) if it is enabled on your account. ⁠ Depending on your chosen security type, you will be prompted to enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator), a code sent via SMS to your registered phone number, or a code sent to your registered email.
Click "Sign In." You will be directed to your Personal Area dashboard, where all your Real and Demo trading accounts are listed. From here you can navigate to deposits, withdrawals, account settings, and the Exness Terminal. ⁠
Google Login
If you originally registered your Exness account using the "Sign up with Google" option, you can also log in via Google. ⁠ On the sign-in page at my.exness.com, click "Continue with Google," select the Google account you used during registration, and authorize the connection. ⁠ You will be logged in to your Personal Area without typing a separate password. ⁠

It is important to note that Google login only works if your account was created through that method. You cannot link an existing email-registered account to Google login after the fact. Even when logging in via Google, Exness still enforces your 2FA security method for sensitive actions like withdrawing funds or changing security settings. ⁠

How to Log In via the Exness Trade Mobile App
The Exness Trade app is available on both Android (Google Play Store) and iOS (Apple App Store). ⁠ It combines Personal Area management and mobile trading into a single application. ⁠

Download the official app from the App Store or Google Play. ⁠ Verify that the publisher is listed as Exness — do not install apps from third-party sources. ⁠
Open the app and tap "Sign In" (not "Register"). ⁠
Enter your registered email address and your Personal Area password — the same credentials you use on my.exness.com. ⁠ You can also tap "Continue with Google" if your account was created that way.
Complete 2FA verification if prompted.
Set up your app passcode and biometrics. During your first mobile login, the app will prompt you to create a 6-digit PIN and enable biometric authentication — fingerprint recognition on Android or Face ID / Touch ID on iOS. ⁠
On subsequent logins, you can unlock the app in under one second using your fingerprint or face, without re-entering your full credentials. ⁠ The biometric data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to Exness servers, adding a hardware-based verification layer on top of your password and 2FA protections. ⁠
Inside the app, you can also choose your preferred trading terminal under Profile → User Settings → Trading Terminal — either the native Exness Terminal or the built-in MetaTrader 5 interface. ⁠

How to Log In to MT4 or MT5 (Desktop and Mobile)
MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 use entirely different credentials from the Personal Area. Here is how to log in:

Desktop (Windows / Mac)
Open the MT4 or MT5 application on your computer. ⁠
Click File → Login to Trade Account (MT4) or File → Open an Account (MT5). ⁠
Enter your numeric trading account number (Login ID). You can find this on your account card in the Personal Area under Trading → Accounts.
Enter your trading password (not your Personal Area password).
Select the correct server from the dropdown list. For live accounts, the server name typically looks like "Exness-MT5Real14." For demo accounts, it looks like "Exness-MT5Trial8."
Click Login.
Mobile (Android / iOS)
Open the MT4 or MT5 mobile app.
Go to Settings → New Account → Login to an Existing Account.
Search for "Exness" in the broker search bar and select the matching server. ⁠
Enter your Login ID, trading password, and confirm the server.
Tap OK / Sign In. ⁠
Exness Terminal (Web)
The Exness Terminal is Exness's proprietary web-based trading platform. ⁠ It does not require a separate login — you open it directly from the Personal Area after signing in. Navigate to Trading → Accounts, select your account, and click to open the terminal. No additional password or server name is needed. ⁠

Security Features of Exness Login
Exness provides multiple layers of protection to keep your account secure:

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): You can enable 2FA through Settings → Security Settings → 2-Step Verification in the Personal Area. ⁠ Three methods are supported:
Authenticator App (TOTP): Google Authenticator or any compatible TOTP app generates 6-digit codes on your phone. This is the recommended method because it works offline, does not depend on SMS delivery, and protects against SIM-swap attacks.
SMS Verification: Exness sends a code to your registered phone number via SMS, WhatsApp, or Telegram. Convenient but less secure, as codes can be intercepted or delayed on poor networks.
Email Verification: A code is sent to your registered email address. Useful as a fallback but slower. ⁠
If you lose access to your authenticator app or phone, you can reset your security type to email from the Personal Area settings. ⁠
Password Requirements: Personal Area passwords must be at least 8 characters and include both letters and numbers. Trading passwords can be set to your own specification. ⁠
Account Lockout: If you fail login 5 times, your account is automatically locked for 30–60 minutes to prevent brute-force attacks. ⁠
Phishing Protection: Exness will never ask for your password, OTP, or financial details through Telegram, WhatsApp, Facebook, or any other messaging platform. ⁠ The only legitimate login pages are my.exness.com and the official Exness Trade app. ⁠ If you receive an email with a login link claiming to be from Exness, do not click it — go directly to my.exness.com instead.
Biometric Login on Mobile: Fingerprint and face recognition add a hardware-based verification layer that ties access to your physical device. The biometric data is stored locally and never sent to Exness servers. ⁠
Common Login Issues and How to Fix Them
Forgot your Personal Area password: Use the "Forgot password?" link on the sign-in page at my.exness.com. Exness will send a password reset link to your registered email address. Click the link, set a new password, and use it to log in.
"Authorization Failed" in MT4/MT5: You are almost certainly using your Personal Area password instead of your trading password, or you selected the wrong server. Go to the Personal Area, open Trading → Accounts, and check the correct Login ID, trading password, and server name on your account card. ⁠
2FA code not working: Make sure your device's clock is synchronized, as TOTP codes are time-sensitive. If you have lost access to your authenticator app, reset the security type to email from the Personal Area settings. ⁠ If SMS 2FA consistently fails, switch to an authenticator app for more reliable delivery. ⁠
Login page looks wrong or different: You may be on a phishing site. Always type my.exness.com manually into your browser or use a saved bookmark. Never log in through links in emails, social media messages, or search advertisements. ⁠
Google login not working: This only works for accounts originally registered via Google. If you registered with email and password, you must use that method. ⁠
App won't open or crashes: Make sure you are using the latest version of the Exness Trade app from the official app stores. Clear the app cache or reinstall if the problem persists.
Tips for a Smooth and Secure Exness Login Experience
Bookmark the official URLs (my.exness.com) in your browser for quick and safe access.
Enable 2FA with an authenticator app as soon as possible after registration. ⁠ It is the single most effective step you can take to protect your account.
Save your Google Authenticator recovery key in a safe place. If you lose your phone or the app, this key is the only way to recover access without contacting support.
Do not reuse your Exness password on other websites. Use a unique, strong password for your trading account.
Log out of all devices when you are done, especially on shared or public computers.
Check for planned maintenance on the Exness website before troubleshooting login issues, as scheduled maintenance can temporarily interrupt authentication services.
Never share your 2FA codes with anyone, including someone claiming to be Exness support. ⁠
Is Exness Login Available in India?
As of July 2025, Exness stopped accepting new registrations from Indian IP addresses, and support has confirmed there are no plans to resume. However, existing accounts remain fully operational. If you already have an Exness account, you can log in, trade, deposit, and withdraw without restrictions using the same methods described above. Be aware that funding an offshore trading account from India falls under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), and domestic payment methods like UPI are generally not supported. Bank transfers are the typical funding method, and some Indian banks may flag or block such transactions.
